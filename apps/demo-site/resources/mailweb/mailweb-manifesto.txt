THE ENTIRELY UNNECESSARY MAILWEB MANIFESTO

The Internet is not HTTP.
A document is not its delivery mechanism.
A link may be a request for private correspondence.
A page may arrive with its stationery already folded around it.
And if the page needs a picture, the picture can fit in the same letter.

No CDN was contacted in the making of this attachment.

MailWeb is deliberately absurd.
That is the point.
